The problem today

Every late bus turns into a round of phone calls.

  • Students wait without knowingIs the bus late, already gone, or not coming? From a stop, there's no way to tell.
  • The office becomes a call centreTransport staff spend the morning answering the same question.
  • Drivers get distractedThe only person who knows where the bus is, is the one driving it.
  • Nothing is on recordBreakdowns and delays get handled by phone and forgotten.

What Busszo is

One live view, shared by everyone who moves your students.

  • Transport adminSets up buses, routes and people, and watches every bus live from the web console.
  • Your staffDispatchers and office staff, each given only the parts of the console they need.
  • DriversStart the trip with one tap; the app shares the bus location until they end it.
  • StudentsSee their own bus on the map and get alerts as it gets close.

When plans change

Closed roads, breakdowns and absent drivers — handled in the app.

Road closed

Suspend a stop for the day

The office marks the stop closed — or names a temporary one.
Students at that stop see it in their app, with the nearest open stop.
Reopen it later; everyone's stop assignment is still there.

Bus in trouble

Breakdown, traffic, weather

The driver reports it from the app in one tap.
Students on that bus and the office are alerted at once.
Cleared: students hear the bus is back on the road.

Driver or seat change

Switch drivers and students

Move a driver to another bus, or students between buses.
The bus keeps its route and its stops.
Moved students are notified: "Assigned to bus 14".

Getting started

From sign-up to buses on the map in five steps.

  1. Your officeRegister your collegeCreate the account; we verify it before it goes live.
  2. Your officeAdd buses and routesBus numbers, capacity, and each route's stops on the map.
  3. Your officeImport drivers and studentsUpload your existing lists from a spreadsheet.
  4. Your officeAssign them to busesA driver per bus, and each student to a bus and a stop.
  5. Drivers & studentsInstall and sign inMobile number and a one-time code. The next trip is live.

No devices to buy and nothing to fit on the buses: drivers and students use the phones they already carry, and the office uses any browser.
